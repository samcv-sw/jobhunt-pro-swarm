Job Seeker Quick Start Guide

5 Easy Steps to Automate Applications & Land Interview Callbacks

STEP 1

📄 Upload CV & Build Candidate Profile

Go to Upload CV →

Upload your resume (.pdf, .docx) and complete your candidate profile information (Name, Email, Phone, Target Profession, and Technical Skills).

🤖 Crucial Action: Scroll down to the bottom of the page and click "AI Optimize & Launch" (or Save Profile) to let AI structure your profile for ATS matching.
STEP 2

🎯 Target CV Profile & Initialize Swarm

Select Target →

Select your active Target CV Profile and double-check that your exact preferred CV is selected for company submissions.

⚡ Crucial Action: Choose your desired campaign package/tier and click "INITIALIZE SWARM" to activate your target company rules.
STEP 3

💳 Fund Wallet (Crypto Deposit Min $6)

Add Funds →

Add funds to your wallet using Crypto (USDT, BTC, ETH - Minimum $6 deposit) or Credit Card.

💡 Pro Tip: Your wallet balance fuels real-time SMTP dispatching, AI cover letter customization, and automated follow-up sequences.
STEP 4

⚔️ Battle Station & Live Dispatches Tracking

View Dispatches → Battle Station →

Navigate to your Battle Station and click "▶ START ALL". Your selected active campaign will immediately appear under the Active Campaigns section.

📬 Live Application Tracking: Under "Dispatches" (Sent Emails), candidates can view the exact list of target companies, job titles, dispatch status, recipient email addresses, and preview the full cover letter payload submitted to each company!
STEP 5

🔌 Laptop Power Settings & Background Execution

⚠️
CRITICAL ACTION: Open your Laptop / PC Power & Battery Settings. Ensure Power / Sleep mode is set to "NEVER" so your computer never goes to sleep or pauses application submissions!
💻
Multitask Freely: Keep the dashboard tab open or minimized in the background. You can freely use your PC for work, gaming, or browsing while the AI Swarm automatically applies for jobs in real-time!
⏳
Track Remaining Time: Watch the "Campaign Time Remaining" counter to monitor campaign progress.

Employer & Recruiter Quick Start Guide B2B Hiring

Complete step-by-step walkthrough to post jobs, accept candidate applications, and fund your recruiter workspace.

STEP 1

📢 Post a New Job Opening & Set Up Requirements

Post a New Job →

Navigate to "Post a New Job" (/post-job). Enter your exact Job Title, Required Qualifications, Industry, Salary Range, and Company Name.

📧 Crucial Step for Employers: Enter your official Company Contact Email. This is the exact email address where candidate applications and CVs will be dispatched!
🎯 Choose your listing tier (Standard Posting, Featured Employer, or AI Swarm Sourcing) and click "Publish Job Opening".
STEP 2

💳 Recruiter Wallet & Crypto Payment (Min $6)

Employer Wallet →

Fund your employer workspace using Crypto (USDT, BTC, ETH - Minimum $6 deposit) or Credit Card.

⚡ Why Crypto Deposit? Crypto deposits trigger instant balance credits with zero banking holds, enabling immediate AI candidate screening, featured job placement, and automated job promotion blasts across candidate networks.
STEP 3

📬 Track Applicant CVs & Recruiter Email Delivery

Track Applicant CVs →

Easily view and manage all candidate applications submitted to your job openings.

📥 Direct Email Ingestion: All candidate applications, attached PDF resumes, and custom AI cover letters are sent directly to the Company Contact Email you specified during job posting.
📊 Live Recruiter Portal: Go to "Track Applicant CVs" (/employer/track) and enter your email to access a real-time tracking dashboard showing candidate match scores, submission timestamps, and full candidate profiles.
STEP 4

🔌 Keep Dashboard Active & Automate Candidate Sourcing

⚠️
POWER SETTINGS: Keep your browser tab open or minimized. Set your Laptop/PC Power & Sleep settings to "NEVER" so your automated candidate sourcing and notification loops run continuously.
🤖
AI Candidate Sourcing: The JobHunt Pro AI engine continuously screens thousands of candidates 24/7, auto-matching qualified talent to your open roles in real time!
